      How to play

      1. Trace a word. Drag across touching tiles (diagonals count). Each tile once per word. Or tap tiles one by one and tap the last one again to cast.
      2. Score. Each letter has a value. DL doubles a letter, 2× doubles the whole word, and words of 6+ letters earn a +10 bonus.
      3. Collect crystals. Tiles with a give you a crystal (max 10). Spend them on Shuffle (1), Swap a letter (3), or a Hint (4). Powers don't end your turn.
      4. Win. Everyone gets one word per round. Highest score after the last round wins.
